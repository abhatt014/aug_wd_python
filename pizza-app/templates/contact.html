Before you write

Do I need an account to order?

Yes. Create one once with your phone and address, and every order after that takes a few taps.

How far do you deliver?

Free delivery within 5 km of the outlet. If you're further away, the shop may reject the order and tell you why.

Why is my order still waiting for approval?

The shop checks every order before it goes in the oven. If it's been more than 10 minutes, call us.

Can I pay online?

Not yet. We take cash on delivery only.
