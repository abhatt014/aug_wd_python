Pizza ordering app.

Every screen of the Crust & Co. ordering system, ready to turn into Flask templates. Click any screen to try it. Nothing is sent to a server.

Try it as:

Try the whole order

  1. As a visitor, open the home page, add a few pizzas and press Go to checkout. You're asked to log in first.
  2. Log in as rahul@example.com (any password of 6+ characters), check the address and press Place order.
  3. Switch to the shop owner above (or log in as admin@crustandco.in). Open the dashboard, then accept or reject the new orders.
  4. Open order #1048 and move it through Preparing, Out for delivery and Delivered.
  5. Download the revenue report as CSV and open it in Excel.
