Before you write

How far do you deliver?

Free delivery within 5 km of the outlet. Up to 8 km we charge ₹40. Beyond that, call us and we'll see what we can do.

My order is late or wrong. What should I do?

Message us on WhatsApp with your name and order time. We'll check with the kitchen and fix it, usually with a fresh pizza or a refund.

How early should I book a party order?

At least 24 hours ahead for up to 20 guests, and 3 days ahead for larger groups. Choose "Party or bulk order" in the form above.

Do you have Jain or gluten-free options?

We make Jain pizzas (no onion, garlic or root vegetables) on request. We don't have a gluten-free base yet because our kitchen handles flour all day.
